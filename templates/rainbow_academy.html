Rainbow Academy

Rainbow Academy provides education for around 190 children — from pre-school through the years leading into secondary education — serving both children from Sunbeam Children's Home and the wider local community.

Learning Alongside Community

The school was built to make sure no child in the home grows up without an education, and to open that same opportunity to children from the surrounding villages. Bringing children from the home and the community together in one school strengthens ties between KSM and the people it serves.

What Rainbow Academy Offers

  • Pre-school through upper primary education
  • Preparation and support for children transitioning into secondary school
  • A learning environment rooted in Christian values
  • A place where children from the home and the local community learn side by side
