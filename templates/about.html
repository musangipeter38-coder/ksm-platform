About Kenya Sunbeam Ministries

Kenya Sunbeam Ministries (KSM) is a Christian faith ministry working in the Teso District of Western Kenya — a beacon of light in a rural part of Africa that has known deep hardship.

Our Story

KSM was founded by Ellen Allison, a missionary who has served in Kenya since 1998. Formerly a nurse in Belfast, she left full-time employment in Northern Ireland to dedicate her life to this work, trusting God to provide for her needs.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, faith-centered home for vulnerable children — orphaned, abandoned, neglected, or living with disabilities — and to nurture them from birth through age 18 with care, education, and hope.
