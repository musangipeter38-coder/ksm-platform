Sunbeam Farm

Sunbeam Farm is KSM's path toward greater self-sufficiency — raising animals and growing food that helps sustain the children's home, the school, and the wider community.

Working Toward Self-Sufficiency

Rather than relying only on outside support, KSM has developed a working farm with cows, goats, and chickens, alongside crops of fruit, vegetables, grain, and oil. This reduces the ministry's dependence on donations for daily needs and teaches practical skills to the young people who grow up here.

What the Farm Supports

  • Daily food for children at the home and school
  • Practical agricultural skills for young people
  • Reduced dependence on outside food donations over time
  • A model of sustainability the wider community can learn from
