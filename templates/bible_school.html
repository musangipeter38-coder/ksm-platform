Shalom Bible School & Local Church

The Shalom Bible School and local church meet at the KSM Prayer Centre, which also hosts Sunday School lessons and Holiday Bible Clubs for children in the home and the community.

A Well-Attended Church

Ellen Allison, KSM's founder, serves as the local Pastor. Under her leadership, a well-attended church has grown at the Prayer Centre, offering worship, teaching, and discipleship for adults and children in the Teso District.

Growing in Faith

  • Sunday services open to the whole community
  • Sunday School for children
  • Holiday Bible Clubs during school breaks
  • Bible School teaching for those pursuing deeper discipleship
