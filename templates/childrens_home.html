Children's Home & Refuge Centre

Sunbeam Children's Home is a safe refuge for children who have been orphaned, abandoned, neglected, or abused — placed with us by local authorities in the Teso District of Western Kenya.

A Family, Not Just a Shelter

Every child who comes through our doors is met with care, structure, and the love of a real family — not just a bed and a meal. Children grow up here from infancy through age 18, supported through school, faith, and community.

What We Provide

  • Housing, food, and daily care
  • Access to Rainbow Academy for education
  • Christian discipleship through the Shalom Bible School & Church
  • Counselling and support for children recovering from trauma
